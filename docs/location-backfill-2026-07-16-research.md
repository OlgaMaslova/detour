# Verified location backfill research — 2026-07-16

## Method

The roster and IDs are the 29 Paris records seeded by `1767976000_add_michelin_2026_paris_two_three_starred.js` plus the two still-unlocated Madrid records in the existing Michelin import. Addresses were checked on 2026-07-16 against a current first-party restaurant/hotel page; where a first-party page did not expose a usable address, the address source is the official Michelin individual restaurant record. Coordinates were obtained independently from OpenStreetMap through Nominatim, with Overpass used only for the two Madrid host footprints that are not indexed as named/addressed venues.

`osm_verified` means the selected OSM element names the restaurant (or, for Alléno Paris, the restaurant's published Pavillon Ledoyen identity). `osm_address_verified` means there is no suitable named restaurant element: the point is exact-address geometry or explicitly identified host-premises geometry. It must not be interpreted as an entrance or dining-room centroid. Every OSM element and any side-street, duplicate, host-building, or untagged-footprint relationship is stated below and in the CSV audit note. All sources were accessed **2026-07-16**.

In the table, **V** is the venue's official URL and **A** is the address source.

## Caveats and rejected ambiguity

- OSA and Pabú remain absent as named OSM venues. Their coordinates are OSM building-centre geometry, selected because each first-party site's own embedded location point falls inside the footprint. Neither footprint carries the restaurant name or street address in OSM, so both are deliberately `osm_address_verified`, not `osm_verified`.
- Plénitude, Hakuba, L'Abysse, and L'Orangerie use named host-premises geometry. L'Oiseau Blanc uses the exact Peninsula street-address node. These are not restaurant-room or entrance coordinates.
- Kei, Blanc, Table, and Virtus use exact OSM house/address nodes because no unambiguous named venue element was found.
- Explicitly rejected same-name/duplicate candidates are recorded for Épicure, Alliance, L'Oiseau Blanc, and L'Ambroisie. No ambiguous result was used.
- The authoritative backfill artifact is [`data/verified-location-backfill-2026-07-16.csv`](../data/verified-location-backfill-2026-07-16.csv); every CSV field is quoted.
